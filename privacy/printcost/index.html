Privacy policy for Whizzed Print Cost

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Whizzed Print Cost is an offline 3D-print cost calculator for Android (“Whizzed Print Cost”, “Print Cost”, “the app”), published by Whizzed. Whizzed is a trading name of DGFIX LTD. The app’s package name is uk.whizzed.printcost. This policy explains what information is used when you use Whizzed Print Cost.

Who is responsible

The organisation responsible for this app and for this policy (the data controller) is:

DGFIX LTD (trading as Whizzed)
Company number: 10646551 (registered in England and Wales)
Registered office: 13 Chestnut Road, Dartford, Kent, DA1 2LB, United Kingdom
Email: Holusa.d@seznam.cz

If you have a question, want a copy of the information we hold, or want us to delete what we can, please email that address.

A short summary

  • Whizzed Print Cost is an offline 3D-print cost calculator. You can use it without creating an account.
  • DGFIX LTD (trading as Whizzed) does not collect any data itself. We do not run a Print Cost login or a Print Cost server that stores your calculations.
  • If ads are on, the app shows a Google AdMob banner. Those ads are not personalised.
  • You can make a one-off “Remove ads” purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and we do not receive your card details.

Information stored on your device

Whizzed Print Cost works offline on your device. Any figures you enter and any results the calculator shows stay on the device. This information does not go to DGFIX LTD’s own computers or servers.

If you uninstall Whizzed Print Cost or clear the app’s data in Android settings, this local information is deleted. You can also ask us for help with that using the email above.

After you buy Remove ads, the app only learns from Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so it can hide ads and so you can restore the purchase after reinstalling.

Ads (Google AdMob)

If ads have not been removed, Whizzed Print Cost uses Google AdMob to show a banner advert.

We ask AdMob to show non-personalised ads only. That means interest-based advertising and remarketing are switched off.

Google’s advertising software typically receives some technical information so that an ad can be delivered, so that the same ad is not shown too often (frequency capping), so that fraud and abuse can be limited, and so that Google can produce aggregated reporting. That can include:

  • the device’s IP address (which can suggest a rough location, such as a city or region, not a precise GPS position), and
  • device identifiers.

Google acts as a separate organisation (a third party) when it provides these advertising services. Google’s own advertising privacy information is at: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

If you buy Remove ads, the app stops asking for ads. After that, these advertising requests should stop.

Purchases (Remove ads)

Remove ads is a one-off in-app purchase, paid through Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes the payment details. Whizzed Print Cost and DGFIX LTD do not see or receive your card details or your Play account password. The app only learns from Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so it can hide ads and so you can restore the purchase after reinstalling.

Google’s payments and privacy terms also apply to the purchase. See Google Play’s documentation and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information.

We do not collect data ourselves. Information is shared only as follows:

  • Google, when ads are shown (AdMob), and when you buy or restore Remove ads (Google Play Billing).
  • The law, if we are required to.

We do not have our own advertising partners or analytics company besides what is described here.

How long information is kept

  • Information on the device stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Information that Google collects for ads or payments is kept by Google under its own policies. We do not control those periods.
  • If you email us, we keep the email long enough to answer you and to deal with any follow-up, then we delete it when we no longer need it.

Your rights

If UK or EU data protection law applies to you, you may have the right to ask for a copy of personal information, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object or restrict certain uses, and to complain to a regulator.

For the United Kingdom, that regulator is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk
For the EU, you can contact your local data protection authority.

To make a request about Whizzed Print Cost, email Holusa.d@seznam.cz. Because we do not collect data ourselves, and any calculator information lives only on the device, the most effective way to delete it is often to clear the app’s data or uninstall it. We will still help if you write to us.

Security

Information that leaves the device towards Google is sent using encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS), according to Google’s documentation. No method of transmission is perfect, and we cannot guarantee complete security.

International transfers

Google may process advertising and payments information outside the United Kingdom or the EEA. Google is responsible for the safeguards it uses for those transfers, as described in its privacy policy.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the “Last updated” date and, where we can, the copy in the app. The current version will always be the one on this page.
