This website

This website is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no contact forms, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not set cookies.

Like any website, the hosting provider that serves these pages processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, so that the site can be delivered and protected against abuse. We do not use that information to identify you.

If you email us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and deal with any follow-up, and we delete the email when we no longer need it.

Your rights

If UK or EU data protection law applies to you, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to complain to a regulator. In the United Kingdom that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk. To make a request, email Holusa.d@seznam.cz.
