Privacy policy for Flashsums

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Flashsums is a mental maths and brain-training game for Android (“Flashsums”, “the app”), published by Whizzed. Whizzed is a trading name of DGFIX LTD. This policy explains what information is used when you play Flashsums. Flashsums is meant for players aged 13 and over.

A short summary

  • You can play Flashsums without creating an account. There is no Flashsums login, no Flashsums server and no analytics.
  • Your scores, local Top 10, streaks and settings are stored on your device.
  • If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your leaderboard scores and your Play Games name are sent to Google’s Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see them.
  • Flashsums shows no ads. It does not use Google AdMob and does not use advertising identifiers.

Information stored on your device

Flashsums keeps the following in the app’s own storage on your device:

  • scores, best results, your local Top 10, the Daily Brain Mix result and streak,
  • your settings, such as sum types, brain modes, time limits and sound.

This information does not go to DGFIX LTD’s own computers or servers. Sound effects are generated on the device. If you uninstall Flashsums or clear the app’s data in Android settings, this information is deleted.

Global leaderboards (Google Play Games)

When Google Play Games leaderboards are switched on in the app and you are signed in to Play Games, your leaderboard scores and your Play Games name are sent to Google’s Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see them. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy, and you can manage or leave Play Games in your Google account settings. Only scores from standard settings are sent. If you are not signed in, or the leaderboards are not switched on, everything stays on this device.

Signing in to Play Games is optional. You can play every mode without it, and the local Top 10 keeps working.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Flashsums is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and think we hold information we should not, email sulikovazena@gmail.com.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. Information is shared only with:

  • Google, for Play Games leaderboards if you sign in,
  • the authorities, if the law requires it.

How long information is kept

  • Information on your device stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • Leaderboard scores held by Google are kept under Google’s own policies. You can manage Play Games data in your Google account.
  • If you email us, we keep the email only for as long as we need it to reply and deal with any follow-up.

Your rights

If UK or EU data protection law applies to you, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to complain to a regulator. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk. To make a request, email sulikovazena@gmail.com.

Security and international transfers

Information sent to Google travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Google may process it outside the United Kingdom or the EEA and is responsible for the safeguards it uses for those transfers.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when Flashsums changes how it uses information, and we will change the date at the top.
