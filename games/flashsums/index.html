Flashsums

11+ maths and reasoning practice: timed sums, verbal and non-verbal skills.

Flashsums is a fast neon maths and brain trainer for quick, timed practice of the skills used in the UK 11+ (eleven plus) and in KS2 maths. Race the clock on mental maths, from Easy to Expert, then switch to brain modes that practise verbal reasoning and non-verbal reasoning — word, letter, shape and sequence puzzles.

You can play in portrait or landscape. There are global Play Games leaderboards, and a local Top 10 on your device. No account is needed to play. Sign in to Play Games only if you want the global boards.

Flashsums is skill practice in a game. It is not an official 11+ exam, it does not include full mock papers, and it is not linked to any exam board.
