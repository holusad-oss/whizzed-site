Stellar Run: Space Shooter

A one-thumb arcade space shooter. Sectors, bosses, DRYDOCK upgrades and bombs.

Stellar Run is a portrait arcade space shooter you can play with one thumb. Drag to fly, your guns fire on their own, and each sector ends in a boss. Spend coins in the DRYDOCK shop on weapons, thrusters, hull or a bigger ship.

Flashsums

11+ maths and reasoning practice: timed sums, verbal and non-verbal skills.

Flashsums is a neon maths and brain trainer for timed practice of the skills used in the UK 11+ and in KS2 maths. It is skill practice in a game, not an official 11+ exam, and it is not linked to any exam board.

Meridian: A Quiet Atlas

A calm KS2 geography quiz. Name countries, flags and capitals on a globe.

Meridian is a calm geography quiz for Android. Learn countries through modes such as Globe, Flags, Capitals and Find, with a journey through the world’s regions. Play without an account; progress is saved on your device.
