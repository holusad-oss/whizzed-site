Stellar Run: Space Shooter

A one-thumb arcade space shooter. Sectors, bosses, DRYDOCK upgrades and bombs.

Stellar Run is a fast portrait arcade space shooter you can play with one thumb. Drag anywhere to fly. Your guns fire on their own. Clear waves of enemies, survive the boss at the end of each sector, then spend coins in the DRYDOCK shop — upgrade your weapon, thrusters and hull, or buy a bigger ship. Double-tap or tap BOMB to clear the screen when the belt gets ugly.

Seven themed sectors wait, with a local high score to beat. On your first death in a run you can revive with coins, or by watching an optional rewarded ad. The game itself works offline, and no account is required.

Contains ads (a banner and full-screen ads). A one-time purchase (£0.99) removes those. Optional revive ads can still be watched if you choose. Ads need an internet connection.
