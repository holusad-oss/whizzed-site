## A short summary

- You can play Stellar Run without creating an account. There is no Stellar Run login and no Stellar Run server that keeps your game saves.
- The game itself works offline. A local high score is stored **on your device**.
- The game uses the internet to show the **Top 2 online score board**. If you submit a score, your chosen name, score and country flag are stored and shown to other players.
- The app shows ads served by **Google AdMob** (a banner and full-screen ads). Ads need an internet connection.
- A one-time purchase (£0.99), made through **Google Play**, removes the banner and full-screen ads. Optional revive ads can still be watched if you choose.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Information stored on your device

Stellar Run keeps a local high score, and other settings the app needs to run, in the app’s own storage on your device. This information does not go to DGFIX LTD’s own computers or servers. If you uninstall Stellar Run or clear the app’s data in Android settings, this local information is deleted.

## Online leaderboard (Top 2)

The game uses the internet to show the Top 2 online score board. If you submit a score, your chosen name, score and country flag are stored and shown to other players. Do not use your real name if you don't want it shown.

Submitting a score is optional. The local high score on your device still works if you do not submit.

Stellar Run has no accounts. To delete a leaderboard name and score, email sulikovazena@gmail.com with the subject “Stellar Run data deletion” and the name you used. We delete it within 30 days.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner and full-screen ads, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to revive after your first death in a run.

To show and measure ads, the AdMob software may collect and share with Google:

- your device’s advertising ID,
- device information (such as model, operating system version and IP address), and
- app interaction and diagnostic data (such as ad views, clicks and crash logs).

Google may use this data for personalised ads where you have allowed it. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Ad data held by Google can also be reset or deleted from those settings (for example, Delete advertising ID).

Google acts as a separate organisation (a third party) when it provides these advertising services. Google’s own privacy and advertising information is at: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>, <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>

If you buy **Remove ads**, the banner and full-screen ads are removed. Optional revive ads can still be watched if you choose. Ads need an internet connection.

## Purchases (Remove ads)

Remove ads is a one-time purchase of £0.99, paid through **Google Play**. Google collects and processes the payment details. Google’s payments and privacy terms also apply to the purchase. See Google Play’s documentation and privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. Information is shared only with:

- **Google**, for ads (AdMob) and for Google Play purchases,
- **other players**, if you submit a score to the Top 2 online score board (your chosen name, score and country flag are shown on that board),
- **the authorities**, if the law requires it.

We do not have our own advertising partners or analytics company besides what is described here.

## How long information is kept

- Information on your device stays until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
- If you submit a score, your chosen name, score and country flag stay on the Top 2 board until they are replaced by a higher score, or until you ask us to delete them as described above.
- Information that Google collects for ads or payments is kept by Google under its own policies. We do not control those periods.
- If you email us, we keep the email only for as long as we need it to reply and deal with any follow-up, then we delete it when we no longer need it.
