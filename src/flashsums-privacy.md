## A short summary

- You can play Flashsums without creating an account. There is no Flashsums login, no Flashsums server and no analytics.
- Your scores, local Top 10, streaks and settings are stored **on your device**.
- If you choose to sign in to **Google Play Games**, your leaderboard scores and your Play Games name are sent to Google’s Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see them.
- Flashsums shows ads served by **Google AdMob** (a banner on the menu and leaderboard, and sometimes a full-screen ad after a finished round — never during a timed round). Those ads use the advertising ID.
- A one-time purchase (£0.99), made through **Google Play**, removes the ads. You can restore the purchase from Settings.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Information stored on your device

Flashsums keeps the following in the app’s own storage on your device:

- scores, best results, your local Top 10, the Daily Brain Mix result and streak,
- your settings, such as sum types, brain modes, time limits and sound,
- whether a Remove ads purchase has been restored from Google Play.

This information does not go to DGFIX LTD’s own computers or servers. Sound effects are generated on the device. If you uninstall Flashsums or clear the app’s data in Android settings, this information is deleted.

## Global leaderboards (Google Play Games)

When Google Play Games leaderboards are switched on in the app and you are signed in to Play Games, your leaderboard scores and your Play Games name are sent to Google’s Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see them. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy, and you can manage or leave Play Games in your Google account settings. Only scores from standard settings are sent. If you are not signed in, or the leaderboards are not switched on, everything stays on this device.

Signing in to Play Games is optional. You can play every mode without it, and the local Top 10 keeps working.

Google’s privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Ads (Google AdMob)

Flashsums uses Google AdMob to show a banner on the menu and leaderboard screens and, sometimes, a full-screen ad after a round has finished (never during a timed round).

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and share with Google:

- your device’s advertising ID,
- device and app information (such as model, operating system version, and a device or app identifier such as the app set ID),
- the device’s IP address, which can suggest a rough location (such as a city or region, not a precise GPS position),
- ad interactions (for example that an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostic information.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Google acts as a separate organisation (a third party) when it provides these advertising services. Google’s own privacy and advertising information is at: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>

If you buy **Remove ads**, Flashsums stops asking for ads.

## People in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland

In these regions, Flashsums may show a consent message (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, where that is required. You can change that choice later from Settings when a “Privacy options” entry is shown.

## Purchases (Remove ads)

Remove ads is a one-time purchase of £0.99, paid through **Google Play** (Google Play Billing). Google collects and processes the payment details. Flashsums never sees your card details or your Google account password. It only learns from Google Play whether you own the purchase, so it can turn ads off and so you can restore the purchase after reinstalling. Purchase status is stored on your device and by Google Play.

Google’s payments and privacy terms also apply to the purchase. See Google Play’s documentation and privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
