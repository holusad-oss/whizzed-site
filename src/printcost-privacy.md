## A short summary

- Whizzed Print Cost is an offline 3D-print cost calculator. You can use it without creating an account.
- DGFIX LTD (trading as Whizzed) does not collect any data itself. We do not run a Print Cost login or a Print Cost server that stores your calculations.
- If ads are on, the app shows a Google AdMob banner. Those ads are **not** personalised.
- You can make a one-off “Remove ads” purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment, and we do not receive your card details.

## Information stored on your device

Whizzed Print Cost works offline on your device. Any figures you enter and any results the calculator shows stay on the device. This information does not go to DGFIX LTD’s own computers or servers.

If you uninstall Whizzed Print Cost or clear the app’s data in Android settings, this local information is deleted. You can also ask us for help with that using the email above.

After you buy Remove ads, the app only learns from Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so it can hide ads and so you can restore the purchase after reinstalling.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

If ads have not been removed, Whizzed Print Cost uses Google AdMob to show a banner advert.

We ask AdMob to show **non-personalised** ads only. That means interest-based advertising and remarketing are switched off.

Google’s advertising software typically receives some technical information so that an ad can be delivered, so that the same ad is not shown too often (frequency capping), so that fraud and abuse can be limited, and so that Google can produce aggregated reporting. That can include:

- the device’s IP address (which can suggest a rough location, such as a city or region, not a precise GPS position), and
- device identifiers.

Google acts as a separate organisation (a third party) when it provides these advertising services. Google’s own advertising privacy information is at: <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>

If you buy **Remove ads**, the app stops asking for ads. After that, these advertising requests should stop.
